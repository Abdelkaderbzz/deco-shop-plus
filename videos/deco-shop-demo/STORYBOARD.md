# Deco Shop Plus — Demo Stories

Plan validé. Captions-only · zooms · highlights · musique.

## Video direction

- **palette system** — cream canvas `#E4EBE7`, ink `#1A2220`, accent teal `#0F5C64` (pink-deep), WhatsApp green `#25D366` only on CTA; flat paper depth, 2px hairlines, Montserrat display.
- **motion grammar + reveal model** — long-tail `power3` eases; each caption cue reveals a new visual piece (never dump at t=0); Ken Burns / zoom-to-target under UI shots; highlighter circles on options; hold stillness after last reveal.
- **rhythm / held frames** — Frame 1 & 7 hold reads; Frames 2–5 stay busy with scroll/zoom; Frame 6 brief trust hold before CTA.
- **negative list** — no browser chrome/nav bars as fake UI chrome; no purple AI glow; no front-load-then-freeze; no floating screensaver orbs; caption keep-out bottom 17%.
- **device** — phone mockup (~78% height) centered in portrait for UI frames; cream letterbox around device.
